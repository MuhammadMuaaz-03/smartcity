Rules & Laws

Standards for a fair, orderly city

Clear enforcement frameworks and digital infrastructure standards that keep the city accountable and transparent.

01
🏗️

Infrastructure Standards

Interoperability and safety benchmarks for all city technology deployments. Every system must meet open-standard protocols for seamless integration.

02
🏪

Encroachment Rules

Stall permits and fair-use policies to keep public spaces accessible to all citizens. Digital permit tracking eliminates corruption from the process.

03
🚗

Digital Traffic Enforcement

Automated fines with photographic evidence logs. No human discretion in issuing penalties — the system is transparent and tamper-proof.

04
🗺️

Zoning Regulations

Clear commercial and residential boundary definitions with transparent planning records. All zoning decisions are publicly accessible on the city portal.

Q: How does anonymous reporting work?
All reports can be submitted without personal details. The system uses one-way encryption so even administrators cannot identify reporters.
Q: Who has access to city data?
Aggregated data is open to citizens via the public portal. Personal data is strictly governed by the city's Digital Privacy Charter.
Q: How are smart signals programmed?
AI models analyse real-time vehicle density from sensors and cameras, adjusting signal timing every 30 seconds to minimise average wait times.
